Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my speed test result differ from my contracted plan speed?

Contracted speeds are theoretical maximums measured under ideal conditions. Actual throughput depends on internal LAN wiring, Wi-Fi interference, the number of concurrent users, and peak-hour network congestion. If your measured speed is consistently below 70% of your plan, consider a line inspection or plan upgrade.

What is a good ping for business use?

Under 30 ms is considered excellent for all business applications including VoIP and cloud ERP. 30–80 ms is acceptable for standard web and email. Over 100 ms may cause noticeable lag in real-time collaboration tools and video conferencing.

Should I use Wi-Fi or a wired connection for the test?

For the most accurate result, connect via Ethernet directly to your router or switch. Wi-Fi results are valid but introduce wireless overhead — typically 20–40% lower than wired speeds on the same connection.

What is a dedicated leased line and when do I need one?

A leased line (dedicated line) provides guaranteed symmetric bandwidth exclusively for your business — no contention with other users. It is recommended when your operations require consistent upload speeds, a fixed IP, or SLA-backed uptime (e.g., VPN gateways, hosted servers, point-of-sale systems).
